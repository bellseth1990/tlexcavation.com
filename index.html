Hard-to-reach shoreline? We come by water.

We have two barges that haul material straight to the work area, so we can rebuild a shoreline without setting foot on your property.

  • Less wear on your yard. Trucks and equipment stay off your lawn, landscaping and driveway.
  • More precise rock placement. Working from the water lets us set each stone where it belongs.
  • Faster job, lower cost. Less hauling and less cleanup saves us time and saves you money.

Meet Todd, Jake and Jordan Dolata

Todd Dolata is the owner and operator. He started running backhoes, excavators and graders as a teenager in his dad’s septic and excavation business in 1985, then spent twelve years with Cape Construction in Racine, rising from backhoe operator to superintendent. His jobs there included airport runways, the Milwaukee River restoration and the Menominee River diversion.

In more than 40 years of excavation, Todd has worked with Milwaukee Metropolitan Sewer District, Wisconsin DOT, Milwaukee County Airport Engineering, the cities of Racine, Kenosha and Adams, and Adams County Land and Conservation and Parks and Recreation.

As a small company we know our customers by name, and many of them hire us again. We work with your budget and build a plan that fits it.
